How Our Mentorship Program Works

Step 1
Course Selection
You'll begin by evaluating your current skills, qualifications, and career objectives. This self-assessment helps you recognize strengths and areas for development to choose a path aligned with your goals.
Step 2
Enrolment
Once you've identified the ideal course, secure your spot by selecting your course and completing checkout. You’ll receive confirmation for the next intake to officially start your journey.
Step 3
Orientation
Attend a comprehensive orientation session covering course structure, objectives, and expectations. Meet fellow students and staff to foster a supportive community.
Step 4
Dev Plan
Have a one-on-one meeting with your mentor to develop a customized plan that aligns with your goals and learning style, ensuring a smooth start.
Step 5
Mentorship
Your mentor will meet regularly to guide progress, assist with challenges, celebrate successes, and keep you on track to meet career goals.
Step 6
CV & Interview
Attend a workshop with our lead mentor focused on refining your CV and preparing for job interviews, boosting your confidence to take the next career step.
